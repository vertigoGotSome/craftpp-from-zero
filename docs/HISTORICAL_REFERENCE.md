# Historical reference

## Candidate baseline

Markus Persson announced Minecraft 0.0.11a for public use in May 2009. His TIGSource thread dates to May 17. Classic 0.0.11a is the leading candidate for the earliest playable public release. Secondary chronology describes a short-lived, unplayable 0.0.10a immediately before it and flags that the launcher artifact labelled c0.0.11a appears to represent later development. The exact build and its available evidence remain open. Earlier Cave Game footage documents development before public release.

- [Persson's archived 0.0.11a announcement](https://blog.omniarchive.net/post/109000107/minecraft-0011a-for-public-consumption/)
- [Persson's TIGSource thread](https://forums.tigsource.com/index.php?topic=6273.0)
- [Secondary release chronology and launcher provenance](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_Classic_0.0.11a)
- [Contemporary footage uploaded May 17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LEG9feKeQMY)

## Evidence and rights

Track the source, author, date, build identity, observation, confidence and corroboration for each finding. Record conflicting evidence and adoption or departure decisions, including historical bugs.

A public game release describes distribution of a playable artifact. Publicly viewable source describes access. Openly licensed source requires an explicit license. Decompiled source is a derived reference whose access and use require separate evaluation. Record those distinctions and keep study material outside the public repository. Review provenance and applicable rights before any third-party inclusion.
